Why Specter

A serious workspace for terminals, remote files, and the systems around them.

Specter brings sessions, shells, serial consoles, remote files, and a full text editor into one focused workspace. It is built for the moments between connecting, inspecting, changing, and reconnecting.

Security

Trust the connection

Reduce credential and trust-surface risk by design.

  • Real host-key verification

    Trust-on-first-use checks show fingerprints clearly and warn when a known host key changes.

  • Nothing sensitive in profiles

    Private keys and passphrases are never stored in session profiles, and encrypted bundles exclude passwords and known_hosts.

  • Encrypted BYO-storage bundles

    Export configuration with PBKDF2-SHA256 and AES-256-GCM for OneDrive, Dropbox, S3, or another user-managed provider.

Remote Files

Move files with care

SFTP workflows that respect the files and timestamps you are working with.

  • Open with system apps

    Open remote PDFs, images, and other files through private temporary copies and the operating system's default handler.

  • Preserve timestamps

    Remote downloads preserve modification times, and drag-and-drop uploads carry local modification time when supported.

  • Sidebar and editor alignment

    Pin local folders in the sidebar, open them directly into an editor workspace, and resize editor and terminal panes independently.

Scope

Focused by design

Some surfaces are deliberately outside the current product boundary.

  • Not integrated yet

    VNC and RDP require dedicated graphical client surfaces. Automatic cloud-provider browsing and background sync are not included.

  • Future productivity work

    Full macro recording, replay, and a plugin architecture remain future features.

  • Future tunnel work

    Dynamic SOCKS forwarding and remote SSH forwarding remain planned for a future tunnel manager.
