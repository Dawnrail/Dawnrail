Why Specter

Built for people who live in a terminal all day.

Verification

Real host-key checks

Trust-on-first-use verification, with a clear warning and the fingerprint shown whenever a host key changes, so you can decide whether to trust it.

Everywhere

One terminal, everywhere

The same terminal on Windows, macOS, and Linux, with a standard installer for each platform.

Yours

Wallpaper, fonts, color

Color-scheme presets, a curated font list, a wallpaper behind the prompt. The thing you look at for eight hours a day should look like yours.

Why
Dawnrail is built by a network engineer, for the terminal they actually use during a real SSH session, not a roadmap slide.

Every feature here exists because a real switch, a real SSH session, or a real 2am incident asked for it.
